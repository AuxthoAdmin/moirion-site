Privacy

Your journal. Your choices.

How Moirion handles your journal and your choices.

Free iPhone app

Moirion does not require an account and Auxtho does not collect your journal entries, conversations, contact details, or usage data through the free iPhone app. Entries and conversations are created when you write or talk in the app and are kept on your iPhone. They are used to show your journal, let you find past entries, and answer a question you choose to ask about them.

AI and sharing

The free iPhone app uses Apple's on-device model on compatible devices when it is available. Moirion does not send your journal or questions to Auxtho, an external AI service, or a third-party SDK. Metdol, cloud AI, user API keys, and Moirion's backup and restore are unavailable in this iPhone release. If you contact support, the message and address you choose to send are used to respond to you; support correspondence is separate from the app's journal data.

Storage and deletion

Your iPhone entries and conversations remain on the device until you delete them in Moirion, reset the app's data, or remove the app. Moirion does not retain a server copy of this data. iOS device backups, if you have enabled them, are controlled by your Apple settings and may have separate retention and deletion rules. You can stop using the AI feature at any time by not starting a conversation; writing and reading remain available.

Mac and optional connections

The Mac app also starts with local records. You may configure a compatible local model or explicitly choose an optional connection. Before a Metdol preview sends a question to your chosen computer, Moirion shows the destination and asks you to confirm. These optional Mac connections are separate from the free iPhone release described above.

Support

For product questions or data-related requests, contact moirion@auxtho.com.
