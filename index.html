Before you start

Common questions.

What is Moirion?

Moirion is a lightweight private journal. Write first, then use local AI to talk with a day or entry when you want to revisit it.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start using Moirion locally without creating an account.

Does my data leave my computer?

Your journal stays on your device by default. You choose what to export and which connections to use. In the Metdol preview, you confirm each question before it goes to your paired PC and choose whether to include recent conversation.

Do I need a local model before I start?

You can write, browse, and open your entries straight away. Add a compatible local model when you want to talk with your journal.

What does local AI mean?

The model runs on your own computer. Moirion connects your questions with a compatible local model, so you can keep the conversation on your Mac.

Which languages does Moirion support?

The interface is available in English, Korean, and French. English is the primary AI language; Korean and French AI conversations are Alpha. Conversation quality also depends on the model you choose.

Can I use my own PC for AI?

The Metdol preview connects Moirion on Mac with a paired Windows PC on your local network. Choose Metdol for a question, confirm what you are sending, and receive the reply right in Moirion. Your Mac remains the default.

Device and AI setup

Mac builds: Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Writing, browsing, and searching work independently of AI. For conversations, install Ollama and a compatible local text model on your Mac, then choose it in Moirion. Memory and storage needs depend on the selected model.

iPhone builds: iOS 17 or later for the journal. Apple on-device AI requires iOS 26 or later, an Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the on-device model ready. Moirion shows model availability in the app.

AI languages: English first. Korean and French AI conversations are Alpha.

These are the current build requirements. Final App Store listings will confirm release compatibility and availability.
